# 🧠 Domi’s Psychology

A revision companion for **Cambridge International AS & A Level Psychology (9990)**, following the official syllabus for 2027. It covers both years, with a **Y12 / Y13 switch**. Each year has its own look and keeps its own notes and progress.

## Year 12: AS Level (Papers 1 & 2)
- **All 12 core studies** across the four approaches (biological, cognitive, learning and social). Each comes with the psychology behind it, the study itself and an evaluation.
- **A full research methods unit** for Paper 2 (18 subtopics), including help with the planning question.
- Paper 1 and 2 exam guide, plus the five AS issues & debates.

## Year 13: A Level (Papers 3 & 4)
- **All 4 specialist options**: Clinical, Consumer, Health and Organisational (60 subtopics and 20 key studies).
- Paper 3 and 4 exam guide, plus the eight A Level issues & debates and the methods toolkit.

## In both years
- Short YouTube videos (over 260 in total), each explaining one subtopic.
- Quizzes, spaced-repetition flashcards, and exam-style questions with answer guides.
- **Listen**: notes are read aloud using the device’s built-in voices.
- **Notebook**: notes tagged by topic, with search, pins and colours, plus export/import.
- Revision planner, search, dark mode, adjustable text size, and offline use.

## Saving
- Progress is saved **separately for each year**: notes, confidence ratings, quiz scores, flashcards, exam answers, exam date and plan. Switching years never deletes anything.
- Your name, theme, voice and streak are shared between the two years.
- Everything is stored in the browser (localStorage). Use *Settings → Download full backup* to move it between devices. The backup includes both years.

No build step is needed: it’s plain HTML, CSS and JS. Content lives in `assets/content-*.js` (Year 13) and `assets/y12-*.js` (Year 12).
